# Project1830

/projects/1830-juno-edition/ — a project page in 1830's identity from masthead to footer: certificate stock, coal ink, red.

1. **ProjectMasthead** (1830): numerals, the rail collage, the six verbs, facts, and PLAY in its in-development state.
2. **Tabs** (sticky): Overview · The game · Development · Notes.
3. **01 Overview** — two paragraphs and a figure (share certificates).
4. **02 The game** — the **MechanicsStack** of 1830's real actions; an illustrative hex **RouteMap** with legend beside the train roster **Ledger**; two **Certificates**.
5. **03 Development** — one honest sentence, the PLAY plate, the facts.
6. **04 Notes on 1830** — rows tagged 1830.

- The page body stays on `p1830-stock` (the field, `data-theme="stock"`), so the site's theme never re-tints the project. Grain only on the masthead.
- Only published facts: what the original game does, that this edition is being built for Juno, and that it is in development. No dates, prices or player counts until they are real.
