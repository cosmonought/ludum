# PullQuote

A sentence lifted out of a note or a page, set in Anton capitals under a short `red` bar.

**Markup.** `<figure class="ld-quote [ld-quote--frame|--s]"><blockquote><p>…</p></blockquote><figcaption>Source</figcaption></figure>`.

- Quote only what the page says elsewhere; never invent a speaker.
- At most one per note. `--frame` (on `frame`, with a `yellow` bar) for the homepage and About; `--s` inside narrow columns.
- Max 22 characters a line; it should read in three or four lines.
