# About

/about/ — what Ludum is, and what its statuses mean. No page head and no eyebrow: the page opens on its first section.

1. **01 What Ludum is** (stock) — the page's h1, straight under the nav with no rule; two paragraphs of prose, with Freud on play as an **Epigraph** in the side column.
2. **Statement** on the frame: "Games are models you can *enter.*", with Badiou beside it (**Statement**, quoted).
3. **02 How statuses work** (a tinted panel, `paper-2`) — the five stamps with one sentence each.
4. **Close** (stock) — Benjamin's line on play as an **Epigraph** panel, centred and large, alone, as the homepage closes on Schiller's.

- **The grounds alternate** (stock, frame, tint, stock), so every boundary is a change of ground and no section draws a rule.

- Tone: plain, confident, specific. No hype words, no "Web3", no "revolutionary".
