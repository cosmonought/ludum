# Certificate

The engraved share plate, drawn in CSS: a ring-guilloche border, the company in Anton, the kind of share in italic, a seal, the value.

**Markup.** `<figure class="ld-cert [ld-cert--cosmos] [ld-cert--tilt]">` → `.ld-cert__band`, `.ld-cert__inner` → `p.ld-cert__top` (three spans), `p.ld-cert__company`, `p.ld-cert__kind`, `.ld-cert__seal`, `p.ld-cert__value` (`b` + a line of terms).

- 1830 plates are coal on `p1830-stock` with a `red` seal; the President's certificate is two shares (20%) of a ten-share company, as in the original.
- 20 Cosmos plates are blue engraving on cream; they are specimens and say so. Never make a plate that could pass for a real security, token or NFT.
- Hover straightens a tilted plate and lifts it 4px.
- Railroad names in 1830 are the original's historical companies (Pennsylvania, B&O, New York Central…). 20 Cosmos companies are invented.
