# MetadataStrip

A ledger line of facts: a 4px rule, then cells of mono key and value divided by hairlines.

**Markup.** `<dl class="ld-meta">` of `<div><dt>Key</dt><dd>Value</dd></div>`; `ld-meta--compact` tightens it inside features. A status in a strip is the small stamp.

- Keys are `label-s` in `ink-3` (`--pj-ink-3` in a project field); values are `data` in `ink`.
- Use it under mastheads and article heads, never as a table of more than five cells.
- Only facts that are true and settled: status, what it is based on, family, network, dates. Unknowns are left out rather than marked "TBA" — except where the absence is the fact ("play.netadao.org, when ready").
- Phone: two columns.
