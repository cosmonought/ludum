# StatusStamp

A rubber-stamp plate that says where a project stands. Every project shows exactly one.

**Markup.** `<span class="ld-stamp ld-stamp--dev">In development</span>`; `ld-stamp--s` for rows and metadata (mono 11px); `ld-stamp--tilt` once per composition, on a masthead or collage.

| Status | Class | Fill / text | Means |
|---|---|---|---|
| PLAYABLE | `--playable` | `red` / `on-red` | Runs on play.netadao.org now |
| IN DEVELOPMENT | `--dev` | `yellow` / `on-yellow` | Being built, in public; follow its notes |
| EXPERIMENTAL | `--experimental` | `blue` / `on-blue` | A playable prototype; rules may change |
| RESEARCH | `--research` | `green` / `on-green` | Study and writing, not a game |
| ARCHIVE | `--archive` | `grey` / `on-grey` | Finished or retired, kept on record |

- The word carries the meaning; the colour repeats it. Never a coloured dot alone.
- The plate is a pigment border, a 2px hairline of the ground (`paper`) inside it, and the word in Anton (19px) or Plex Mono (`--s`).
- Today both games are IN DEVELOPMENT.
- Don't invent statuses ("Coming soon", "Beta", "New").
