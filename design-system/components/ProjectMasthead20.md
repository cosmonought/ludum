# ProjectMasthead20

The 20 Cosmos variant of ProjectMasthead, built like Project 18XX's: "20" over COSMOS with the tagline in tracked mono, a steel-blue engraving bled off the right edge behind them, the themes as a row.

- "20" is `p20c-yellow` at d0; "COSMOS" is `p20c-cream` at d1 beneath it; the tagline "Robber barons of the internet age" is mono at 14px, tracked 0.34em.
- The picture is the glass-tower boardroom (`assets/20 Cosmos/c20-boardroom.webp`): the Project 18XX board meeting a century later, steel-blue line on night navy with the network glowing yellow on the table. Its left third is empty, so the title sits over it; `ld-lineart` fades its left and lower edges into `p20c-night`.
- The RouteMap network chart moves down the page, to **02 The world**.
- The six words — Networks · Capital · Routes · Governance · Coordination · Speculation — are what the game is *about*. They are not mechanics; don't gloss them as rules.
- The internet age, not outer space: no planets, nebulae or starfields, and no trains or rails, on 20 Cosmos pages.
- Phone: the picture bleeds edge to edge as a 4:3 band, cropped to the people at the table.
