# ProjectMasthead20

The 20 Cosmos variant of ProjectMasthead: one line of title, the tagline in tracked mono, the themes as a row, and the network chart across the full width.

- "20" is `p20c-yellow`; "COSMOS" is `p20c-cream`; the tagline "Railways for a multichain world" is mono at 14px, tracked 0.34em.
- The art is the RouteMap network chart, drawn live in SVG; its routes draw themselves once, and pointing at a station lights the routes it touches.
- The six words — Networks · Capital · Routes · Governance · Coordination · Speculation — are what the game is *about*. They are not mechanics; don't gloss them as rules.
- Not outer space. No planets, nebulae or starfields on 20 Cosmos pages: networks are infrastructure.
- Phone: "20" and "COSMOS" stack; the chart crops around the hub instead of shrinking (`ld-map--crop`); its legend panel drops under it.
