# GamePieces

Drawn tokens: the only round things in Ludum (`radius-token`). Company stations, revenue values, a hub.

**Markup.** `<span class="ld-piece [ld-piece--ink|--yellow|--green|--blue|--value]">PRR</span>`, 56px.

- A piece carries an abbreviation or a number in Anton, never a word.
- Pigment fills take their `on-…` text. `--value` is a cream disc ringed in ink, for revenue.
- Use pieces in diagrams, legends and collages; never as buttons or avatars.
