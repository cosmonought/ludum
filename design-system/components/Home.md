# Home

ludum.netadao.org: games first, then what can be played, then the writing.

1. **SiteNav** — Projects · Notes · About, and PLAY to play.netadao.org.
2. **Hero** — the line at d1 ("Serious games for a more *interesting* world."), one sentence on what Ludum is, and a Why games link to About — and beside it the **DeparturesBoard**, so both games are in the first screen.
3. **The games** — a **ProjectFeature** per game, in its own identity, straight after the hero: no section head (the fields introduce themselves).
4. **Notes** — the newest note as a lead, then three rows. (Held back on the live site until real notes exist.)
5. **Close** — on stock, under a 2px rule: one line on play.netadao.org (today, nothing is playable yet) with its out-link, and Schiller's line on play as an **Epigraph**.
6. **Footer**.

- No eyebrow over the hero line, and no button that only scrolls the page: the games begin directly below. Why games lives on About, not on the homepage.

- Order is priority. When a game becomes playable, its feature gains a linked PLAY and its stamp turns PLAYABLE, and the closing line says so.
- Page rhythm: the hero is stock, the two game fields follow each other directly, and the page comes back to stock before the footer.
- Note titles and dates are sample copy.
- Phone: see **HomePhone** — the board sits between the line and the actions, features recompose, the nav becomes a sheet.
