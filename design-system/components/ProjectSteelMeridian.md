# ProjectSteelMeridian

/projects/steel-meridian/ — LUD—01, a project page in Steel Meridian's identity: railways, rivers and industry across the American interior, in blued steel and plat white with one colour, red.

1. **ProjectMastheadMeridian**: STEEL over MERIDIAN (its first I in red), 18HEARTLAND as the tagline, the span engraving, the six themes, facts and the PLAY plate.
2. **Tabs**: Overview · The world · Development.
3. **01 Overview** — what the game is, that its rules aren't published, and the firebox engraving.
4. **02 The world** — the six themes as a **MechanicsStack** ("themes, not rules"); the board; a specimen **Certificate**; the railways as a **Ledger**.
5. **03 Development**.
6. **Next project** — the **NextNote** band on stock, handing on to 20 Cosmos.

- The whole page sits in the Steel Meridian field (`ld-pj--meridian`, `data-theme="press"`).
- **The board** is the game's own map (v0.8r5), drawn as pure land: every buildable hex unlaid in `sm-plat`, open water in `sm-water`, the six rivers in `sm-blue`, off-boards in red, and the row letters and column numbers in `sm-vermilion`. No cities, towns, names or terrain marks. Redraw it from the game's map data when the map changes; never draw another game's map arrangement.
- **The red** is the game's one colour: the glow in each picture, the red I, the range-pole edge, seals and off-boards. No full-height red lines and no red rivers.
- History is theme and flavour here, not a section of its own.
- Facts come from the game's own documents. The railways are candidates until the map and token audit; say so. No dates, prices or player counts until they are real.
- **Names.** The game is Steel Meridian; 18Heartland is its working title and rides as the tagline.
