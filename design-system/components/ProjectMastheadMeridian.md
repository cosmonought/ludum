# ProjectMastheadMeridian

The Steel Meridian variant of ProjectMasthead, built like 20 Cosmos's: STEEL over MERIDIAN with 18HEARTLAND in tracked mono, a steel engraving bled off the right edge behind them, the themes as a row.

- STEEL is Anton at d2 in `sm-text-dim`; MERIDIAN is Anton at d1 in `sm-plat`, its first I set in `sm-red` and drawn a little long (`ld-redi`, scaleY 1.14). The tagline 18HEARTLAND is mono at 14px, tracked 0.34em.
- The red I appears wherever the name is set large: the masthead and the homepage feature. Never in running text, the nav or the departures board.
- The edge is a surveyor's range pole: 22px bands of `sm-red` and `sm-plat`, 10px wide; on phones a 10px stripe across the top.
- The picture is the span (`assets/steel-meridian/sm-span.webp`): a white line engraving of the bridge under construction, truss spans lifted onto brick piers and falsework, a steamboat passing beneath. Its ground is matched to `sm-steel` and its left half is empty, so the title sits over it; `ld-lineart` fades its left and lower edges into `sm-steel`.
- The six words — Steel · Rivers · Freight · Capital · Industry · Land — are what the game is *about*, not its mechanics.
- Phone: the picture bleeds edge to edge as a 4:3 band, cropped to the workers.
