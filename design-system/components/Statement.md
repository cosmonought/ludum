# Statement

One sentence at d1, poster-size: Ludum's line, or a page's thesis.

**Markup.** `<section class="ld-statement [ld-statement--frame]">` → `.ld-wrap` → `p.ld-statement__text`, with the one picked-out word in `<em>` (`red` on stock, `yellow` on the frame).

- Ludum's line is "Play can be a serious business", with *Play* picked out and no punctuation.
- **Quoted**: a reader can stand beside the line (`.ld-statement__grid`: the line in 7 columns, an **Epigraph** in 5, at 22px, on the line's last baseline), as About sets Badiou beside "Games are models you can enter." Phone and tablet: the reader goes under the line.
- One per page, and only where the page has earned it. Never two picked-out words.
