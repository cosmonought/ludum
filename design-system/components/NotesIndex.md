# NotesIndex

/notes/ — Ludum's working notes. The newest as a lead (**NoteEntry** `--lead`), then every note as a row, filterable by kind.

- Kinds: Design notes · Research · Devlogs · Essays (filters wire through `data-ld-filter="note-list"` and each row's `data-ld-status`).
- Every note is tagged with its project; the tag links to the project page.
- Notes are the record of the projects: a status change is written up here first.
- Titles and dates shown are sample copy.
