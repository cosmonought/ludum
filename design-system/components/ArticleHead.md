# ArticleHead

The top of a note: kicker and tag, the title at d1, a one-paragraph lede, and the metadata strip.

**Markup.** `<header class="ld-arthead">` → `.ld-arthead__kicker` (label + `.ld-tag`), `h1.ld-arthead__title`, `p.ld-arthead__lede`, `dl.ld-meta`.

- Titles are sentences in title position: say the claim ("Why Project 18XX is the first game"), not a topic ("On Project 18XX").
- The lede is the note's argument in two sentences at most.
- Phone: the title drops to d2.
