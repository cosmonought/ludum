# DiagramStyle

The rules for drawing maps, charts and routes, shared by both projects. The full components are in **RouteMap** and **Ledger**.

- **Draw, don't picture.** Maps are SVG built from the system's marks; screenshots and photographs of maps are imagery, not diagrams.
- **Marks.** Track: 7px `p18-track` (Project 18XX). Routes: main 7px, branch 5px, rival 5px, proposed dotted, interchain link cased (20 Cosmos). Places: rings (cities, stations), a filled disc with a halo for a hub, bars for towns. Values: Anton numerals in a ring. Companies: a disc with an abbreviation (Project 18XX's stations are `p18-gold`).
- **Type.** Coordinates and station names in Plex Mono 11–13px, tracked; zone names and values in Anton.
- **Fills.** Project 18XX: dark-green land, laid tiles in the tiers yellow → green → brown → grey, off-board areas red, coal country grey — the board's own colours. 20 Cosmos zones: core (blue), alliance (yellow), emerging (cream hatch), frontier (crosshatch), neutral.
- **Geometry.** Flat-topped hexes. Octilinear routes (horizontal, vertical, 45°). Labels never overlap a line.
- **Legends** sit under the diagram, or in a framed panel inside the chart on 20 Cosmos.
- **Honesty.** A diagram that isn't the game's real map says "illustrative" in its caption.
