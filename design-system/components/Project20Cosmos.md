# Project20Cosmos

/projects/20-cosmos/ — a project page in 20 Cosmos's identity: the night of an operating chart, cream linework, a yellow signal.

1. **ProjectMasthead20**: "20" in yellow, COSMOS, the tagline, the six themes, the network chart **RouteMap** (routes draw once; stations light their routes), facts and the PLAY plate.
2. **Tabs**: Overview · The world · Development · Notes.
3. **01 Overview** — what the game is, and that its rules aren't published yet; a pull quote from the project's own line.
4. **02 The world** — the six themes as a **MechanicsStack** ("themes, not rules"), a specimen **Certificate**, the route **Ledger** and the zone legend.
5. **03 Development** and **04 Notes**.

- The whole page sits in the 20 Cosmos field (`ld-pj--cosmos`, `data-theme="press"`).
- Networks are infrastructure, not outer space: no planets or starfields. Zones and stations have abstract names, never real chains.
- Don't describe mechanics until they're published; certificates are marked as specimens.
