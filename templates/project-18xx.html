01 · Overview

Project 18XX is a railway-and-stock game of the 18xx family. Players buy into railroad companies, take the presidency of the ones they hold most of, and run them — laying track, buying trains, paying dividends or keeping the money.

It is being built for play on Juno, at three tables, with house rules the host can choose. Its progress is logged in Notes.

02

The game

Six actions, as the game plays them.

  1. 01Buy

    Shares in the railroads. The largest holder becomes president and runs the company.

  2. 02Sell

    Shares back to the market, and the price falls with them.

  3. 03Operate

    Lay track, place stations, run trains for revenue.

  4. 04Withhold

    Keep the revenue in the treasury or pay it out as dividends. The share price answers either way.

  5. 05Rust

    New trains make old ones obsolete. Whole fleets vanish at a phase change.

  6. 06Bankrupt

    A president who cannot buy a required train pays from their own pocket. Fail that, and the game ends.

03

The tables

Three ways to set the board, each with its own title card.

  • Table 01

    18XX

    The classic game: the map and tile tray as printed.

  • Table 02

    18XX+

    A larger map, suitable for higher player counts or less blocking at lower player counts.

  • Table 03

    18XX+: A Level Playing Field

    A rebalanced map with additional tiles, private companies, and railroads.

04

House rules

The host sets up a game: one of the three tables, and any of four house rules.

Each rule is tagged by what it does to the game — easier, riskier, harder, chaotic — in the game’s own words.
