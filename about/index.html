What Ludum is

Some systems are easier to play than to explain. A market where everyone owns a piece of everyone else; a network that is worth only what it connects; a rule that turns yesterday’s investment into scrap. Read about them and you understand them. Sit at a table and run them for three hours, and you feel them.

Ludum publishes games of that kind: long, social, and exact, about money, infrastructure, and the ways people coordinate. Its first two are 18xx games — the family of railway-and-stock games that Francis Tresham began.

Games are models you can enter.
