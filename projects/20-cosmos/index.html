01 · Overview

20 Cosmos is the 18xx system rebuilt for the internet age, loosely based on the Cosmos ecosystem. Blockchains take the place of the railroad companies, validators the place of trains, and IBC channels, relayers and bridges the place of track.

Its rules are not published yet. This page shows the game’s world and how it looks while it is in development.
