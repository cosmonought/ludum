01 · Overview

Steel Meridian is an original 18xx game set across the American interior between about 1865 and 1930, from the Rockies to the Appalachians. Railways raise capital, lay track, run trains and carry freight, and crossing the great rivers is something a railway has to pay for.

Its rules are not published yet. This page shows the game’s world and how it looks while it is being built.
